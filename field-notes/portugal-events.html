Field Note

23 AUG 2026 6 min read

AV rental is an eligible cost.
Just not for your festival.

Portugal Events applications for 2027 events open 1 September. I went to read the regulation for one specific reason, and found an exception that changes the whole budget.

I went to the portaria for a practical reason. I wanted to know whether audiovisual equipment rental counts as an eligible cost under Portugal Events, because if it does, that changes the conversation I have with a promoter when we are closing a technical budget.

It does. It is on the list, alínea b), with no hedging: aluguer de equipamento audiovisual.

Structure assembly is in there too, accessibility included. And event organisation and management services, with one condition I will come back to.

Then I read the rest.

For music festivals outside Territórios de Baixa Densidade, only communication costs are eligible.

A festival in Porto does not fund the stage. It does not fund the rig, it does not fund the PA, it does not fund the control. It funds the poster.

This is not a detail. It is half the budget leaving the eligible base because of the event type and the postcode.

What the programme is, quickly

45 million euros for events between 2026 and 2028. Run by Turismo de Portugal. The whole country, islands included.

The applicant is whoever organises. Companies, associations, organising bodies. We technical suppliers do not apply for anything. We show up in the budget of whoever does apply, and that is where this concerns us.

2026 is closed. Allocation exhausted, submissions suspended since June. What is opening is 2027.

Three types, three ceilings

Eventos Turísticos Estratégicos. Minimum qualifying investment 500.000 €, support up to 300.000 €.

Associative or corporate events. Congresses, conferences, conventions, workshops, team activities. Up to 50.000 €.

Events of a local or regional nature. Up to 500.000 € per plan, and only through the Entidade Regional de Turismo, inside a single Plano Anual de Eventos.

That third one is the one almost nobody uses, and it is the only door to local scale. You do not apply directly. You make a pedido de enquadramento prévio to the Entidade Regional, and it is the Entidade that submits to Turismo de Portugal. Anyone working in the North talks to Turismo Porto e Norte before looking at Lisbon. The deadline that matters is theirs.

The four words

Event organisation and management services, quando contratados em Portugal. When contracted in Portugal.

It is written there. It is a condition.

Contracting production abroad can take the cost out of the eligible base. Contracting here keeps it in. For a promoter deciding between a national and an international team, this is a budget line, not a matter of taste.

I have never seen anyone use this argument in a proposal. It is an argument.

Uplifts

25% if it is in a Território de Baixa Densidade. 25% if it is in the IATA winter period. They stack.

The State is not asking for events in the interior and out of season. It is paying for them. If you are deciding date and place, decide with that number in front of you.

Dates

1 September 2026, applications open for events in 2027.

30 consecutive days minimum notice before the event starts, for the estratégica, associativa and corporativa types. Consecutive. I saw at least two consultancy sites write working days. That is wrong, and the difference is almost two weeks.

Submission on the SGPI platform.

Two conditions that ruin good applications

It cannot be combined with other Turismo de Portugal support.

It does not apply to later editions of the same event.

The second one is the one that hurts. The programme is there to attract and to launch, not to sustain. Anyone with an annual event who puts Portugal Events in the budget as a fixed line will have a hole the year after, and will have that hole in exactly the year they have got used to the production level.

There is also the obligation to put the VisitPortugal logo on communication materials. Small, but it lands on someone’s desk at the last minute, as always.

What this changes in practice

If half the line items are eligible and the other half are not, the budget stops being accounting and becomes design. Three things I do from now on, and recommend to anyone organising.

Separate early. If technical and communication arrive glued together in a line item called “production”, nobody can demonstrate what is eligible. The separation has to be done before the application, not after.

Specify instead of estimating. “AV: 40.000 €” supports nothing. Equipment list, function, quantity, period. Eligibility is demonstrated at line-item level, and a list like that either exists or it does not.

Decide the type before anything else. The question is not how much you can get. It is where this event falls, because that is what decides whether the technical budget goes in whole, goes in half, or does not go in at all.

Note. This is a production reading. It is not legal or funding advice, and it is not my place to give it. The figures and deadlines come from Portaria n.º 101/2023 na redacção da Portaria n.º 4/2026/1 and from information published by Turismo de Portugal. Always check against the legislation in force before submitting, because this changes.

If what you need is the technical part, that is what we do: structure the production budget so the eligible component is demonstrable, and specify the technical design to the level of detail an application can carry.
