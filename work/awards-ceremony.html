The brief

A production company had a creative direction, a venue, and a supplier list. What they didn’t have was a signal flow that actually worked: the video server, the LED wall, and the lighting console were three separate systems that nobody had confirmed could talk to each other under show conditions.

What we did

We came in at specification stage, mapped every signal path against the real technical requirements of the show, and identified three critical failure points that would have surfaced on load-in day. The system was redesigned around a single control architecture.

How it ran

The show ran identically across two nights.
