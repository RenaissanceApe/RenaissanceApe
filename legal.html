Privacy Policy

This section explains what data we collect through the website, why we collect it, which third-party services are involved, and how we handle it.

Who we are

Lumen and Pixel is a live event production studio based in Portugal. This website is used to present our services, collect project enquiries, and allow visitors to subscribe to our newsletter.

What we collect

  • Contact form submissions, including name, company or organisation, email address, selected service, and project message.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: your email address, the date and time you subscribed, and the exact consent wording you agreed to.
  • Basic technical information required for website delivery, such as the IP address recorded in server logs by our host.

Why we use it

  • To respond to project enquiries and other messages sent through the website.
  • To send newsletter updates to users who explicitly subscribe.
  • To deliver resources or redirect visitors to external purchase pages where relevant.

Legal bases

  • Project enquiries are processed on the basis of legitimate interest, so we can respond to incoming requests.
  • Newsletter subscriptions are processed on the basis of consent.
  • Where purchases happen through Gumroad, payment and checkout data are handled on Gumroad’s side under their own terms and privacy practices.

Third-party services used on this website

No third-party code runs in your browser on this website. Fonts are served from this domain, and there is no analytics, advertising, tracking, or embedded marketing script on any page. The services below are used, but your browser only contacts them if you submit a form or follow a link to them.

  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. Requests, including IP addresses, are processed by GitHub to deliver pages.
  • n8n Cloud — receives contact form submissions and newsletter sign-ups when you submit them, and passes them to us.
  • cal.com — only if you choose to book a call. Booking happens on their site under their own terms.
  • Gumroad — only if you follow a link to our store. Purchases, payment and checkout data are handled entirely on Gumroad's side under their own terms.

How long we keep data

We keep enquiry and newsletter data only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for communication, operational follow-up, or legal and administrative purposes.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or object to certain types of processing, as applicable under relevant law. Newsletter emails also include an unsubscribe option.

How to contact us

For privacy-related requests, use the contact form on the About page.

This website sets no cookies of its own and loads no third-party code into your browser.

  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind is used on this website.
